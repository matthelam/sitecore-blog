# Craft Fragments — transferable external technique

The subset of external writing craft that genuinely improves this blog without fighting its voice.
Harvested from `technical-writing-best-practices` (Xamfonos, 26 principles), the copy-editing
"Seven Sweeps" (sweeps 1–2 only), and the conversion-copywriting "no AI tells" rule. Everything that
targets marketing conversion was deliberately left out.

Use these **only** through the override in `SKILL.md`: when a fragment here conflicts with
`voice-guide.md`, the voice guide wins.

## Kept from the external technical-writing skill

- **Thesis spine.** State the article's core claim in one sentence; check each section serves it.
  (Matt already does this — his bold per-section line is the local version.)
- **Earned solutions / narrative order.** Establish the problem before the fix. Don't present a
  solution the reader has no reason to want yet.
- **Sentence craft.** One purpose per sentence; break long sentences at the next action or
  dependency; parallel structure when comparing items.
- **Signal-to-noise.** Cut genuine filler: "It is important to note that", "In order to" → "to",
  "basically", "as mentioned earlier".
- **Show, don't stack.** Prove a point with one concrete example rather than three abstract
  adjectives. (Matt's line counts and prices already do this.)
- **Example fidelity.** Real filenames, ports, prices — never `hello-world`. (Already native here.)
- **Teaching, not pointing.** Explain why a step matters, not just which button to click.

## Kept from copy-editing (sweeps 1–2 only)

- **Clarity sweep.** Unclear pronoun references, ambiguous statements, sentences doing too much.
- **Voice/tone consistency sweep.** No unintended drift between registers within a section; read it
  aloud to catch it.

## Kept from conversion copywriting — one rule only

- **No AI tells.** Never use the "no X, no Y, no Z" list, sentences that trail into extra
  comma-clauses to sound profound, or the em dash (`—`). These read as machine-written.
- **Contrast pairs: narrowed for this author.** Ban the hook-style reveal where X is a straw man
  nobody holds ("It's not about the tools. It's about the people."). Keep a contrast when both
  sides are concrete and the contrast *is* the argument. This is one of the author's signature
  moves: "It's a scalpel, not a default." / "a specification to implement, not a hypothesis to
  test." / "The first response gives you a cache. The second gives you a caching _decision_."

## Explicitly DISCARDED (do not apply)

- "So What / Prove It / Heightened Emotion / Zero Risk" conversion sweeps, CTAs, objection handling,
  social proof, urgency — this is not a sales page.
- The external skill's blanket bans that fight the voice (see the override table in `SKILL.md`):
  all hedging, all tangents, all "In Summary" conclusions, all first-sentence hedges. These are
  wrong for this author and are overridden, not applied.
