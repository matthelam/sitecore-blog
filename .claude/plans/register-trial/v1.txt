We have started to settle on how we hand context to an agent. Instruction files at the repository root, skills, layered system prompts, a handful of memory approaches. It is a real maturing of the craft, and it is good at one thing: documenting what I will call **Prescriptive Context** - the rules and standing guidance an agent is told to follow.
===
Prescription only goes so far. In practice I keep stepping back into the loop - to ask the right question, to nudge the agent down a different path, to supply a hard-won insight or point it at the right tool at the right moment. That accumulated human experience is a different kind of context: less structured than the rules, more the _ah-ha_ moment that saves the day. I call it **Reflective Context**, and right now it is neither here nor there. We have very sophisticated memory containers, but I believe there is a heap more clarity to surface in how we hook them up.
===
I have to admit, I originally started compiling all of my research and testing into a single massive white paper, but I kept finding issues in my logic or testing, and kept finding actual implementations worth looking into. I have been stuck for the last two months on simply trying to articulate what I have prototyped, and I am now missing out on the latest updates in the field. This is my way of getting the framework articulated, and I will make progressive blogs as updates. I will also blog in a more structured way about each major platform and how it aligns with the Legal Framework. I have to be clear: there are a LOT of tools out there now, all edging closer and closer to my idea - some may even argue it has surpassed my framework. That is fine. I will keep documenting the actual implementation with different platforms at its core, and it will hopefully serve as a useful baseline for us in the future.
===
The ecosystem has stabilised on a well-described structure for _prescriptive_ rules. We also worked out early that an agent needs memory - or something closer to fine-tuning through context. Those are the RAG and vector stores, the LLM wiki, the MCP data sources.
===
I am not challenging that we have those things. I am challenging **the way we reach for them, and the moment at which we do**. We load memory the way we load everything else - up front, indiscriminately, as part of the standing context. That is the wrong time and the wrong shape for experience. **Experience is precedent. It only matters when the rules in front of you have run out.**
