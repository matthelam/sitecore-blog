# Register trial — draft rules (candidates, not installed)

All versions: substance frozen, calibrated hedges kept, coined concepts kept, AU spelling, no ocker.
One shared fix in all three: "when the rules in front of you ran out" -> "have run out" (tense slip
the Hybrid pass missed in the bake-off).

## V1 — Now (Hybrid baseline)
No register change. Hybrid output as produced in the bake-off.

## V2 — Crisp AU
1. Contractions on for pronoun + auxiliary and negatives (I'm, it's, we've, don't, I'll).
   Do not contract noun + has ("the ecosystem's stabilised").
2. Cut formal connectives: as such, in order to, moving forward, additionally, furthermore.
3. Keep short declarative lines and occasional direct address.
4. At most two light Australian words per section (a heap, keen, reckon). Never ocker.

## V3 — Chat-direct (closest to how the author writes to Claude)
Everything in V2, plus:
5. Shorter sentences; fragments allowed for emphasis.
6. Plain verbs over ornament ("we're settling", "hand it", "push").
7. Typos and missing apostrophes from chat are NOT copied.
