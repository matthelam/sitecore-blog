My read: V2 as the default, with one V3-style line per section at most.

V1 is correct but reads like a paper. With no contractions at all, it's more formal than you are.

V2 keeps every idea and hedge, and sounds like you explaining it to a peer. The changes are mostly mechanical: contractions, "I reckon" for "I believe", and dropped stiff phrasing. That makes it easy to write down as a rule.

V3 is closest to how you write to me, and its best lines hit hard ("Wrong time, wrong shape."). It also costs the most:

  • Strings of fragments start to read as a tic, and that pattern is a common sign of machine-written prose.
  • The aside loses some of its warmth.
  • Shortening moved the meaning slightly in a few places (table below).

Your call. If you pick V2 or a V2/V3 blend, its rules go into the voice guide as written, after the next trial.
