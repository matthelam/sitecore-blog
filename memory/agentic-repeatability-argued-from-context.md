In the Agentic Studio series, repeatability is argued from the saved context (the same Brand Kit and Skill on every run), never demonstrated with repeated or multi-run evidence; do not plan or ask for extra runs.

## Context
Viewers will not sit through evidence runs, and skipping them saves recording time.
The claim is that what we see is more repeatable because the context is saved, not that results are literally identical.
Applies to stage scripts, especially Stage 2 (Context).
