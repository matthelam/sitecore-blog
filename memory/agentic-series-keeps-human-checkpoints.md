In the Agentic Studio series, later stages cut the prompts, never the human checkpoints: human-in-the-loop is a SitecoreAI feature to showcase, and context should carry the process so a person new to the platform is guided through it.

## Context
Matt, on stage 2 (Context): 'we definitely reduce the prompts but we don't reduce the Human in the loops as that is a promoted feature in SitecoreAI.' The context defines what needs doing, so the work can be handed to someone without platform experience and, when they prompt, it guides them through the established process.
