The five Agentic Studio stages exist to reveal Agentic Studio's features one stage at a time, so every stage script is planned so each scene serves that stage's feature reveal and its quality point.

## Context
Matt: 'that is the purpose of the 5 stages, we're slowly revealing their features in Agentic Studio as they unveil in the stages. that is our true focus.'
Stage 2 (Context) reveals two features: Brand Kit and Skills.
Its quality point is context engineering as quality in two senses:
- over many runs, because the context is reused;
- within a run, because reuse justifies the time to build more detailed context.
