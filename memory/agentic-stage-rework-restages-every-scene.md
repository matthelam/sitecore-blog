When an Agentic Studio stage's storytelling is reworked, every scene is restaged for the new emphasis; the worked example and its results stay the same, but no scene is assumed unchanged from the recording.

## Context
Challenge: a reordered Stage 2 script marked the run, checkpoints, limits and result scenes as unchanged. Matt: 'the results and example stays the same, but the entire story telling is different, the emphasis is too.'
Stage 2 (Context) emphasis is two sequences:
- Brand Kit: what is in it, then uploading it, then linking it in the prompt.
- Skills: where skills live, what is taken from the Stage 1 prompt into a skill, what else is added because it is reusable and worth the time, then linking it to the prompt.
